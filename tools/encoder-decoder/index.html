Local Processing

All conversions happen inside your browser. Your input and output are not sent to ThreatHawk servers.

UTF-8 Support

Convert regular text, symbols and international characters without relying on basic ASCII-only browser functions.

Encoding Is Not Encryption

Encoded data can usually be reversed without a key. Do not use encoding alone to protect confidential information.
